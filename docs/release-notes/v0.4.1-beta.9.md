# Dimble 0.4.1-beta.9

Ninth beta of 0.4.1, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). One addition and six fixes. If nothing big comes up, the next release is 0.4.1 for everyone.

New:

- fn C opens Control Center while your icons are hidden (#179, thanks @mityeah). macOS ignores its own shortcut then, so Dimble opens it for you. There's nothing to set up. I tested it on macOS 27.3.

Fixed:

- macOS's recording indicator shouldn't stay on after you quit Dimble. It did when Dimble quit in the middle of taking a picture of the bar. If yours is stuck, `killall replayd` in Terminal clears it.
- Old icons or a pill shouldn't stay at the left of the bar with a Live Activity up. It happened when an app launched just as Dimble took its picture of the bar. I tested it by forcing the two together, so tell me if you still see it.
- Two Dimbles shouldn't run at once anymore. Quitting and opening it again right away could leave two.
- Set Menu Bar Layout keeps the place of apps that aren't running. Setting the same layout again moved them to the left end of their section.
- Shortcuts actions and `dimble://` links don't drag an icon while you hold a mouse button. They went ahead after 8 seconds. The icon moves once you let go.
- With the clock in Always Hidden, Apply doesn't light up with nothing to move.
