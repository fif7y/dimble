# Dimble 0.4.1-beta.3

Third beta of 0.4.1, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Six fixes.

Fixed:

- A recording you hold down in Telegram shouldn't stop on its own now (#159, thanks @openforfuture). When the mic came on, Dimble could put its Camera & mic icon back in its spot with a quick drag. That drag let go of the button you were holding. It now waits until the button is up. I tested it with the media icon, not Telegram itself, so tell me on the issue if a recording still stops.
- An icon macOS keeps right of the « doesn't show twice during a reveal anymore (#157, thanks @mr-steveryan). With Apple's Siri or Control Center in Hidden, the icons moved over when it came back. The animation still showed the bar as it was, so Wi-Fi showed up twice. Those reveals play macOS's own slide now.
- Smooth slides on the hover after a Show All reveal, and on hovers a few seconds apart (#154, thanks @hanin-mohammed). With a Live Activity up, those used to fade instead.
- With Hidden out, a click or middle click for Always Hidden shows Always Hidden alone (#149, thanks @openforfuture). It used to open beside Hidden. The divider between the two sections only shows when both are out.
- Always Hidden out on its own doesn't ask you to Apply anymore (#149). Its icons sit where Hidden's do, and Dimble read that as icons out of order, so "Apply (3)" showed on every reveal.
- An icon macOS parks on its own « should get a real picture (#153, thanks @2f4vwwb5t6-gif). Dimble read it as in plain sight and kept a picture of the chevron as its tile. Old pictures are dropped once, so a few tiles show the app's icon until a new picture is taken.

Under the hood:

- The CLA check on pull requests runs a pinned action, and some code nothing used is gone (#161, thanks @mr-steveryan).
