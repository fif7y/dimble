# Dimble 0.3.4-beta.3

Third beta of 0.3.4, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). More fixes, mostly for Mission Control and Live Activities.

Fixed:

- Opening Mission Control no longer flashes your hidden icons for a split second (#108, thanks @garanda21). And when an app adds or removes its menu bar icon during a Live Activity, Mission Control doesn't show your hidden icons as it opens or closes either.
- While a Live Activity shows, the « flips when you show your hidden icons, the way it does the rest of the time (#107, thanks @artemc). It kept pointing left.
- An icon set to show only when it matters, like Battery when it runs low, stays up to date during a Live Activity. Its percentage stayed where it was when the activity started, and a second « could show up beside it.
- The panel keeps its icon pictures fresh through a long Live Activity. Each open used to try for new ones and come back empty.

Also:

- When an app's icon goes missing from the bar, Dimble's log now says where the app runs from and whether macOS still lists the icon, to help track down #115 (thanks @abonorahGitHun).
