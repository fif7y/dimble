# Dimble 0.4.0-beta.4

Fourth beta of 0.4.0, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Four fixes for things you see rather than things you set.

Fixed:

- Right-clicking the menu bar no longer flashes macOS's own menu before Dimble's (#140, thanks @efilo1). Dimble wasn't taking that click, only watching for it, so Apple's one-item menu opened on the way down and Dimble's landed on top a beat later.
- Coming back to the bar right after leaving it no longer waits half a second (#141, thanks @efilo1). The reveal was waiting on a cover picture the conceal had only just started taking, and that wait couldn't finish in time. Hovering back in cost 447–542ms instead of the usual 70.
- Clicking a hidden item in the panel opens its menu in the one case where it didn't (#142, thanks @alexandertsukanov). Dimble can hold a picture of the bar over the real one, and that picture was taking the click meant for the item. Once it stopped, the press still gave up on a menu that had already opened. It was counting windows instead of watching for the new one.
- No black edge past macOS's own camera and mic pill on the cover. Dimble paints over that pill by repeating the column just left of it. Taking the picture lights the recording indicator, which slides the right end of the bar while the shot is going. The column could land inside the pill and repeat its black out to the edge.
