# Dimble 0.3.4-beta.4

Fourth beta of 0.3.4, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Three fixes, two of them for Live Activities.

Fixed:

- During a Live Activity, clicking an icon in the panel or the row opens that app's menu again. It opened the Live Activity's card instead.
- Dimble's own icons in Hidden, like Shortcuts, now show with the rest of your hidden icons during a Live Activity. They didn't before.
- When the clock is hidden, clicking its spot opens Notification Center right away, even while you record your screen (#103, thanks @TigerCat-hu). Once in a while it opened a second late, and your next click closed it.
