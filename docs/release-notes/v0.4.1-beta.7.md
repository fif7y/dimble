# Dimble 0.4.1-beta.7

Seventh beta of 0.4.1, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Nine fixes.

Dimble is in Homebrew now: `brew install --cask dimble`. The cask installs the stable release, betas stay here.

Fixed:

- Icons out of order at a double-click go back in place (#171, thanks @bortolatto-creator). With both hidden sections out, a Hidden icon could stand among the Always Hidden ones, and the line between the two sections sat past it. Dimble only saw it while the bar was open, waited for the bar to close, and then couldn't see it anymore. It now remembers what it saw and puts it back once the bar shuts.
- A popover's icon no longer keeps its grey highlight after the bar hides (#172, thanks @mr-steveryan). A click elsewhere closed the popover and hid the bar on the same click. The popover was still fading, so the icon left the bar still highlighted. The bar now waits for the popover to go, at most 0.6 seconds.
- The MacBook's bar is read at its full height on models where it's taller than the notch area (#156, thanks @avocadoattack). Dimble measured 40pt of a 45pt bar there, took the bar's own window for another app's and left it out of every picture, icons included. This should fix the fuzzy Row icons on the built-in display, and the panel sitting 5pt too high.
- A Row tile Dimble has no sharp picture for shows the app's icon instead of a blurry enlarged one (#156). System items and Dimble's own buttons draw their own symbol there.
- Icons in the Row sit centred in their tiles again (#156, thanks @avocadoattack). On a 1x display the bar's shadow can leave a faint line along the bottom of an icon's picture. Dimble counted that line as part of the icon, so the icon sat off-centre in its tile. The line is now left out.
- A recording in Telegram no longer stops when the Stop Recording pill moves (#159, thanks @openforfuture). Dimble moved the pill while the record button was still held, and the move ended the recording. Automatic moves now wait until the mouse button is up.
- A click on a menu bar app's icon right after a reveal opens its menu and leaves it open. For about half a second after the icons move, macOS reports that spot as empty bar. Dimble read the click as "hide" and put the section away under the open menu (seen with Velja, Herd, Snib and Bitwarden).
- Switching the main display no longer leaves every bar on Hidden until a relaunch. Dimble kept positions measured on the old main display, so its covers were placed off screen. It now measures again when the main display changes.
- When an icon can't be taken from the sharper bar, the log says why (#156). It only said how many. This is for reports, nothing changes on screen.
