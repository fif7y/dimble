# Dimble 0.3.4-beta.6

Sixth beta of 0.3.4, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). A quick follow-up to beta.5 for clicks on the bar while a Live Activity shows.

Fixed:

- Clicking a Live Activity could do nothing. Its card opens again, and a second click closes it.
- Right-clicking the empty menu bar did nothing while a Live Activity showed. It opens Dimble's menu again.
- Clicking the empty menu bar did nothing while a Live Activity showed. It shows your hidden icons, like it does the rest of the time.
- Battery or Wi-Fi sat too close to the Live Activity beside them. They keep the same gap the pills keep from each other.
- A Live Activity should no longer show twice for a few seconds after the bar shifts.
