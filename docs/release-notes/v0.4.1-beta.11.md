# Dimble 0.4.1-beta.11

Eleventh beta of 0.4.1, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Three fixes, all with a Live Activity up. If nothing big comes up, this is what goes out in the next stable release.

Fixed:

- Now Playing and the other Live Activities show again while an app is full screen on another display (#156, thanks @avocadoattack). Since beta.8 a full-screen app on any display kept them off on every display. Now only the full-screen display goes without. When you move the pointer to the top of that display, they step aside for about 10 seconds. The hidden icons there can show for a moment as its bar comes down.
- The space next to an Always Hidden icon among your Hidden ones closes with a Live Activity up. Beta.10 hid the icon but left its space blank, so your Hidden icons stood apart from the Live Activity. They're drawn moved over now, and a click on one opens the real icon.
- That icon also goes back to Always Hidden once the Live Activity ends. Before, Dimble couldn't tell it was out of place.
