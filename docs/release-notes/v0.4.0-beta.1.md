# Dimble 0.4.0-beta.1

First beta of 0.4.0, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). macOS 27.3 moved Apple's camera and mic pill, so Dimble follows it. Each Live Activity gets its own switch. And Dimble now works out how your macOS behaves by trying it, instead of reading its version number.

New:

- Each Live Activity can be turned off on its own, in Settings › Menu Bar. The switch is kept by the name macOS gives the pill, so your choice outlives the activity, and the list stays there when nothing is running. Apple's camera and mic pill is never on that list, no switch may hide a privacy indicator.

Changed:

- Dimble works out what your macOS does by trying it once and remembering the answer, instead of checking which version you're on. Apple moves this behaviour in point releases, so a version check would need an update from us every time, and another one to undo it when Apple puts something back. Nothing is written down, each launch measures again.

Fixed:

- The Camera & mic item opens Apple's panel again on macOS 27.3. It opened and closed and left you in Privacy & Security. macOS renamed that pill, gave it a new id every camera session, moved its panel to another process and rebuilds it while the bar settles, so Dimble looks for it by shape now and finds it again on each try.
- Apple's camera and mic pill stays on the bar while your icons are hidden, on 27.3. It was painted over, which is a privacy indicator gone.
- Dimble's own camera icon steps aside while Apple's pill shows, instead of the two standing next to each other.
- A second, frozen camera pill no longer sits on the concealed bar beside the live one.
- A Live Activity's picture is no longer taken while its card is open, so the pill can't keep the open look once the card is gone.
- A pill whose text stopped moving gets a quick refresh instead of waiting five minutes, so a paused timer can't sit there with the wrong numbers.
- A Live Activity that macOS couldn't read three times comes back. Locking your screen could take a pill away until the activity ended.
- Focus, the Clock's timer, SharePlay and Live Activities keep up after macOS restarts its logging. Dimble waits a little longer each time one of those readers stops, and the wait never went back down, so after a few restarts a Focus change could land a minute late for the rest of the session.
