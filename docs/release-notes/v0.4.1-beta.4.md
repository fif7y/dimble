# Dimble 0.4.1-beta.4

Fourth beta of 0.4.1, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Twelve fixes and one change.

Fixed:

- Now Playing should come back right after you resume (#156, thanks @avocadoattack). After a pause its pill went away, and Dimble only looked for it again a minute later. With every other Live Activity turned off it didn't look again at all. It now looks as soon as something plays or pauses. I measured it on macOS 27.3 only, so tell me on the issue if it's still late on 27.2.
- Without Screen Recording, the Row and Panel show each app's icon instead of an old picture of the bar (#156). Dimble kept the pictures it took earlier and couldn't take new ones, so they stayed up as fragments.
- A spacer keeps the width you set, and goes down to 1 pt (#155, thanks @defrilitus). It went back to 14 pt at each launch and each reveal, so yours may change size with this update. macOS adds about 16 pt of its own around every icon, so a 1 pt spacer still takes 17.
- An icon macOS keeps right of the « doesn't show twice when the icons hide either (#157, thanks @mr-steveryan). beta.3 fixed the reveal, the hide still played Dimble's picture of the bar. It plays macOS's own animation now.
- A Live Activity isn't drawn half-way through its slide anymore (#164, thanks @fontme). Dimble could take its picture of Now Playing while another pill was still sliding in beside it. It takes a second one right after, and starts over if anything moved.
- Live Activities don't leave a lighter rectangle on the bar after the wallpaper changes (#165, thanks @fontme). Dimble retakes its pictures when the wallpaper or the appearance changes.
- macOS 27.3's "System & App Protection Override" pill shows its count, and it's off by default (#163, thanks @Titelive). It lists every app that can control others, so on a Mac with Dimble it's up all day. Settings › Menu Bar › Live Activities brings it back.
- Two Live Activities from one app keep their own content. Two Sports games could swap scores.
- Settings stays behind the app you're in when Dimble moves icons (#159, thanks @openforfuture). It used to come to the front, even while you were typing somewhere else.
- The Panel tab's "Show hidden icons in" works on a display that has its own choice in Settings › Displays. The cards looked selected and changed nothing there.
- A mouse's back and forward buttons don't count as a middle click on the chevron anymore.
- An app with a named icon doesn't get an extra "Item-1" tile in the editor.

Changed:

- Settings › Menu Bar lists Live Activities and "Show when it matters" before App launchers and Dimble items.
