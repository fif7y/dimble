# Dimble 0.4.1

> **This is the last release before 1.0.0.** I'm freezing the code this week to get 1.0.0 ready, so nothing new goes in, only fixes. If something in 0.4.1 doesn't work for you, open an issue on GitHub, that's what this week is for.
>
> **Your menu bar works with Shortcuts.** Twelve actions get your layout, set it, move icons, show and hide them, and open Search. `dimble://` links do the smaller changes from Alfred, a script or a browser (#169, #146, thanks @rebdeg and @ssbodda-powern).
>
> **More ways into Always Hidden.** A middle-click on the chevron can show it, and so can its own keyboard shortcut. Both are off until you set them (#149, thanks @openforfuture).

Eleven betas went into this one. Dimble is also in Homebrew now, `brew install --cask dimble`. Most of the rest is fixes, for the Row's icons, Live Activities, full menu bars and full-screen apps.

New:

- Dimble has Shortcuts actions (#169, thanks @rebdeg). Get Menu Bar Layout gives back your whole layout as text, and Set Menu Bar Layout puts every icon where that text says, in one go. It also takes that text saved as a file, and the same text works on another Mac. Others move or open an icon, add or remove a separator, and show or hide the hidden icons. You can also turn reveal on hover or on click on and off, and open Search.
- `dimble://` links do the smaller changes without Shortcuts (#169). `dimble://move?icon=Tailscale&to=hidden` moves an icon, and there's `show`, `show-all`, `hide`, `toggle`, `add-separator` and `remove-separator`. Any web page can open a link, so links never read anything back, click an icon, change a setting or set the whole layout.
- `dimble://search?q=Tailscale` opens Search with the text typed in, for Alfred and the like (#146, thanks @ssbodda-powern).
- Middle-clicking the chevron can reveal your hidden icons (#149, thanks @openforfuture). Settings › Behavior › Reveal › "Middle-click reveals" offers Hidden and Always Hidden together, or Always Hidden on its own.
- A keyboard shortcut shows Always Hidden alone (#149). It's in Settings › General › Shortcuts as "Show Always Hidden only". The same key puts it away.
- A Shortcut button's icon can be picked in Settings (#162, thanks @Hydrogenxy). macOS keeps a shortcut's own icon in a folder only Full Disk Access opens, so Dimble can't show that one.
- fn C opens Control Center while your icons are hidden (#179, thanks @mityeah). macOS ignores its own shortcut then, so Dimble opens it for you, however long you hold the keys. There's nothing to set up. I tested it on macOS 27.3.

Changed:

- The line between Hidden and Always Hidden is off unless you turn it on (#171, #149, thanks @bortolatto-creator and @openforfuture). If you liked it in 0.4.0, it's in Settings › Behavior › Reveal, "Show a line between Hidden and Always Hidden".
- macOS 27.3's "System & App Protection Override" pill is off by default (#163, thanks @Titelive). It lists every app that can control others, so on a Mac with Dimble it's up all day. Settings › Menu Bar › Live Activities brings it back, and it shows its count now.
- Settings › Menu Bar lists Live Activities and "Show when it matters" before App launchers and Dimble items.
- The log says when a Time Machine backup starts and ends (#167, thanks @brianaweeks1). It's to find out why the Time Machine icon set to "While backing up" doesn't come out for everyone.

Fixed, the Row and Panel:

- The Row's icons should be sharp on every screen (#156, thanks @avocadoattack). Dimble took every picture from the main display, so with a 1x display as main the MacBook's Row was scaled up and fuzzy. Each screen now draws pictures from a bar of its own scale. An icon with no sharp picture shows the app's icon instead. On MacBooks whose bar is taller than the notch area, the bar is read at its full height, which should also fix a panel sitting too high. I don't have that setup here, so tell me on the issue if yours still looks soft.
- Icons in the Row sit centred in their tiles (#156). On a 1x display the bar's shadow could count as part of the icon.
- An icon over a sharp edge in your wallpaper gets its own picture in the Row and Panel (#156). Dimble read the edge as another icon in the way and showed the app's icon instead.
- Icons macOS parks behind its « should get their real picture (#153, thanks @2f4vwwb5t6-gif). Their tiles kept an old picture or a chevron. Old pictures are dropped once, so a few tiles show the app's icon until a new one is taken.
- Without Screen Recording, the Row and Panel show each app's icon instead of old fragments of the bar (#156).
- Time Machine and Dimble's other icons open their menu with the icon in the bar (#167, thanks @brianaweeks1). From the Panel, the menu opened with the bar fuzzy and the icon missing.
- Pressing an icon in the Panel works in a full-screen app with the menu bar hidden. Docked, it also opens the right icon when macOS's « has it.
- The Panel tab's "Show hidden icons in" works on a display that has its own choice in Settings › Displays.

Fixed, Live Activities:

- Now Playing should come back right after you resume (#156). It could take a minute, or not come back at all with every other Live Activity off. I measured it on macOS 27.3 only.
- A Live Activity isn't drawn half-way through its slide anymore (#164, thanks @fontme).
- The bar doesn't keep a lighter rectangle or band after the wallpaper changes (#165, thanks @fontme).
- The pill shouldn't sit in a lighter or darker box after you move to another display (#156).
- An Always Hidden icon that ended up among your Hidden ones stays hidden with a Live Activity up, and goes back to Always Hidden once it ends. Battery showed all through a call on a display that shows every icon, and each time you hovered to show the hidden icons. It can still flash for a second as the Live Activity starts.
- Live mode steps aside on a display where a full-screen app has the bar (#156, thanks @avocadoattack). It stayed up as a block at the top of a full-screen video for about 13 seconds. Your other displays keep their Live Activities. When you move the pointer to the top of the full-screen display, they step aside for about 10 seconds, and the hidden icons there can show for a moment as its bar comes down.
- The pill doesn't blink when you hover the bar, and a reveal doesn't cut it before macOS's «. Hidden icons also show when the « was left open.
- Two Live Activities from one app keep their own content, two Sports games could swap scores. Your other displays show the same pills as the main one.
- Old icons or a pill shouldn't stay at the left of the bar with a Live Activity up. It happened when an app launched just as Dimble took its picture of the bar. I tested it by forcing the two together, so tell me if you still see it.

Fixed, reveal and hide:

- Smooth and Fade play with a Live Activity up (#154, thanks @hanin-mohammed). Your icons came and went in one cut whatever style you'd picked. Smooth also survives a clock that changes width and quick hovers. I filmed it on macOS 27.3 only, if you're on 27.2 and the slide ends on a small jump, tell me on the issue.
- An icon macOS keeps right of the « doesn't show twice during a reveal or a hide (#157, thanks @mr-steveryan).
- The icons should hide after a hover reveal even when Dimble didn't see the pointer leave the bar (#162, thanks @Hydrogenxy). I couldn't make it happen here, so tell me on the issue if yours still sticks.
- A tooltip or an open menu holds the reveal, wherever you've left the pointer (#148, thanks @openforfuture).
- A popover's icon no longer keeps its grey highlight after the bar hides (#172, thanks @mr-steveryan).
- A click on a menu bar app's icon right after a reveal opens its menu and leaves it open. Dimble could read it as a click on the empty bar.
- A display's "Hides icons" or "Shows all icons" applies at once with a Live Activity up.
- Switching the main display no longer leaves every bar on Hidden until a relaunch.
- Show in Bar from Search brings a Hidden icon out on a display that hides it, while another display always shows Hidden.

Fixed, your layout:

- Icons out of order at a double-click go back in place (#171, thanks @bortolatto-creator). Dimble only saw it while the bar was open and couldn't anymore once it shut. Apply also reaches Hidden icons that sit behind macOS's « when Always Hidden is out.
- Apply waits until it can read the bar. macOS can stop listing its icons for a few seconds, and an Apply in that moment dropped your changes and moved nothing.
- A recording you hold down in Telegram doesn't stop on its own (#159, thanks @openforfuture). Dimble could move an icon while the button was held. Automatic moves wait for the button now. Settings also stays behind the app you're in when Dimble moves icons.
- An Apply in a full-screen app no longer marks an app as one Dimble can't move.
- When Dimble opens macOS's « to place an icon, the first click should land (#150). A button you add while the bar is full goes to its place, not behind the «.
- A spacer keeps the width you set, and goes down to 1 pt (#155, thanks @defrilitus). It went back to 14 pt at each launch, so yours may change size with this update.
- Dimble tells an app's unnamed icons apart. Stats puts five in the bar without names, and Dimble read them as one. Hiding one of them alone isn't possible yet, macOS hides a whole app at a time.

Fixed, stability:

- With several displays, Dimble could stop responding until you quit it. It asked macOS to watch the menu bar again at almost every reveal. It asks once per launch now, which makes the hang much less likely.
- macOS's recording indicator shouldn't stay on after you quit Dimble. It did when Dimble quit in the middle of taking a picture of the bar. If yours is stuck, `killall replayd` in Terminal clears it.
- Two Dimbles shouldn't run at once anymore. Quitting and opening it again right away could leave two.
- The log names the app or tool that asked Dimble to quit (#130, thanks @cookie-drummer), on a line starting `stop: asked to quit`.
