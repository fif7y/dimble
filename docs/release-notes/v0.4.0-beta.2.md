# Dimble 0.4.0-beta.2

> **The Live Activities item tells you what's running.** Click it and you get the list, newest first, including the ones macOS wouldn't fit on the bar. Your Mac draws two pills and no more, whatever else is going on.

Second beta of 0.4.0, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Two activities from one app no longer open each other's card, and a Wine app no longer eats your memory. That one's out as 0.3.5 too.

Changed:

- Clicking the Live Activities item opens the list of what's running, newest first, instead of bringing the pills back on its own. macOS draws two pills on the bar and no more, so the rest sit under a line that says how many it left off. The two it does draw open their own card from the list, the same as clicking the pill does.

Fixed:

- Two Live Activities from one app each open their own card. Two games in a sports app both call their pill the same thing, so a click on the right-hand one opened the left one's card.
- Dimble's own camera dot no longer flashes on and off while Apple's camera and mic pill arrives, on macOS 27.3. Apple builds that pill, throws it away, then builds it again a quarter of a second later. Dimble read the gap as no pill at all, and it waits that out now before showing its own.
- Dimble no longer climbs a folder path forever looking for the app behind a menu bar icon (#133, thanks @ZeLebo). Wine puts a doubled slash in the paths it writes, and the climb up one of those never reached the top. It took a core, grew by gigabytes a minute, and your icons wouldn't hide.
