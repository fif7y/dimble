# Dimble 0.3.4-beta.5

Fifth beta of 0.3.4, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Accent colors, more ways for Battery and Wi-Fi to come back when they matter, and fixes for the panel's icons and Live Activities.

New:

- Dimble has an accent color now, in Settings › General (#123, thanks @noahweidig). Pick one of six colors, or choose your own with a color wheel, a hex field or the eyedropper. It changes right away with no relaunch, and purple stays the default.
- Wi-Fi can come back while your Mac is on a hotspot, so you can see when it's still on your phone after you get home (#121, thanks @brijazz). Pick "When it drops or on a hotspot" on its row in Settings › Menu Bar.
- Battery can come back while it's charging, or below 30% or 40% (#126, thanks @querevs). There's also "While charging or below 20%", for both at once.

Fixed:

- A window taking focus while the panel took its icon pictures left a line under the tiles in row view and pushed icons up inside them (#127, thanks @jaylotw).
- A tile could show a piece of the front app's menu in place of its icon, when the icon sat under those menus (#127, thanks @jaylotw).
- Screen Mirroring's blue highlight no longer shows up as a square box on its tile (#125, thanks @qbosen). The icon takes your accent color instead while it's in use.
- While a Live Activity shows, hovering or clicking the « shows your hidden icons only on the display under the pointer. It also showed them on your other displays.
- Your hidden icons no longer flash for a frame when a Live Activity ends.
- A Live Activity that had already ended could come back after a relaunch and stay on the bar for hours.
- The Live Activities item no longer sits beside the pills it stands in for. It only shows without Screen Recording or with Show Live Activities off.
- Settings › Menu Bar now names the Dimble items that keep Apple's Now Playing, camera controls, AirDrop, Focus and Timer hidden. It gave the wrong reason if you only had Quick Note, Shortcuts or Fast user switching on.
- Dimble should no longer crash about 2 seconds after launch, while it reads the menu bar.
- The Timer item should no longer stick at 0:00 after a Clock timer ends, which also held your next timer at 0:00.
- The Camera & mic item should drop its SharePlay icon when Control Center restarts during a session.
- A section's separators and Dimble items should no longer disappear until a relaunch over a long session.
- The play/pause item should go back to play when nothing answered your click.
